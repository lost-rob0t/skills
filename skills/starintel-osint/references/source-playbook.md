# Extensive OSINT source playbook

Select the families relevant to the target. Breadth means covering distinct evidence surfaces, not repeating the same syndicated result.

## Identity and entity records

- government registries, corporate registers, beneficial-ownership disclosures, charity/nonprofit filings;
- official biographies, staff directories, board rosters, professional licenses, election records;
- stable identifiers: registration numbers, LEI, CIK, FEC IDs, award IDs, case numbers, usernames, domains, and archived URLs;
- historical names, mergers, subsidiaries, addresses, officers, agents, and jurisdiction changes.

## Government, money, and law

- legislation, regulations, agendas, minutes, votes, budgets, audits, inspector-general reports, and public-record indexes;
- procurement portals, award notices, contracts, grants, spending data, lobbying disclosures, and campaign-finance filings;
- court dockets, opinions, complaints, indictments, judgments, sanctions, enforcement releases, and administrative actions;
- securities filings, annual reports, investor materials, ownership disclosures, and bankruptcy records.

## Organizations and networks

- official leadership, membership, partner, donor, sponsor, conference, and advisory-board pages;
- university, think-tank, foundation, trade-association, standards-body, and conference archives;
- event programs, speaker lists, calendars, meeting records, biographies, and archived organizational charts;
- relations must state the exact predicate, time scope, source, and identity basis.

## Web and technical surfaces

- current and archived websites, sitemaps, robots files, feeds, PDFs, document metadata, and change history;
- source repositories, commits, releases, issues, package registries, SBOMs, and public configuration;
- DNS, certificate transparency, RDAP/WHOIS, ASN/BGP, hosting, and public service metadata when relevant;
- preserve observation time: infrastructure and web content drift quickly.

## Media and discourse

- original interviews, speeches, transcripts, press releases, podcasts, hearings, and social posts;
- reputable local, trade, investigative, and national reporting with attention to common-source dependence;
- fact checks and corrections; trace quotations and factual assertions back to the earliest available primary record;
- social content is attributed publication evidence, not automatic proof of the underlying claim.

## Research and datasets

- peer-reviewed papers, preprints, theses, institutional repositories, data catalogs, and methodology appendices;
- dataset manifests, codebooks, release notes, coverage dates, collection methods, exclusions, and licenses;
- reproduce derived values from the cited source rows or queries when practical.

## Query expansion

Combine exact and variant names with identifiers, roles, predicates, dates, locations, domains, filenames, and source-specific operators. Search both current and historical terminology. Translate names and key terms when the jurisdiction requires it.

For every material gap, record the query, source family, date range, result count or outcome, access failure, and next-best source. A negative search describes the checked scope; it does not prove absence outside that scope.
